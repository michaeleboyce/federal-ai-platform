# Verification pass: US state/local AI adoption programs (2026-07-07)

Dedicated second pass after the deep-research round produced zero
surviving state claims. All access dates 2026-07-07; primary sources
read directly (including the full 52-page PA report). **Headline: every
state time-savings figure is a self-reported survey estimate; the
strongest MEASURED state numbers are adoption/enrollment counts.**

## Pennsylvania — central-platform pilot → expansion (OpenAI)

Primary: "Lessons from Pennsylvania's Generative AI Pilot with ChatGPT"
(March 2025, OpenAI/Commonwealth co-branded):
https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/oa/documents/programs/information-technology/documents/openai-pilot-report-2025.pdf
- VERIFIED: 175 employees / 14 agencies / 5 cohorts / 12 months; 599
  surveys; 48% had never used ChatGPT; 136 of 175 provided feedback;
  >85% positive experience.
- **The famous "95 minutes/day": verbatim "Users ESTIMATED saving 95
  minutes per day"** — an exit-survey self-estimate from a
  non-representative volunteer cohort, GROSS of the paired stat the
  citations always drop: "35 average daily minutes SPENT using ChatGPT."
  Report's own caveats: not representative, not predictive; adoption
  ran at ~half participants' own predictions; 4% never used it.
- Post-pilot (PA OA press release 2026-04-15,
  https://www.pa.gov/agencies/oa/newsroom/shapiro-admin-expands-safe-responsible-ai-improve-services):
  3,000+ employees using GenAI across 35 agencies; 6,500 more enrolled
  in training. (Two operational stats on that page — 400K documents
  scanned/30% flagged, UC chatbot +65% — methodology unstated; verify
  before citing.)

## New Jersey — training-first, state-BUILT assistant (LibreChat)

- NOT OpenAI-powered; state-hosted open-source LibreChat build. Launched
  2024-07; relaunched 2026-03.
- VERIFIED (nj.gov + innovation.nj.gov): 4,000+ active users AND 4,000+
  trained within ~2 months of launch; ~20,000 cumulative users, 300K+
  sessions, 1M+ prompts as of Feb 2026; ~$1/user/month vs ~$20
  commercial (state-estimated savings); training program adopted by 25
  states (Route Fifty corroboration; ~14K monthly users, ~20% of
  workforce signed in).
  https://www.nj.gov/governor/news/news/562024/approved/20240703a.shtml ·
  https://innovation.nj.gov/projects/ai-assistant/ ·
  https://www.route-fifty.com/artificial-intelligence/2025/08/how-new-jerseys-ai-assistant-saves-state-time-and-money/407538/
- DOL-35%-faster and ANCHOR-50% claims: state-reported, methodology
  unspecified — verify before citing.

## California — procurement sandbox (RFI2), thin published outcomes

- VERIFIED mechanism: RFI2 ($1 sandbox testing) + first GenAI
  deployments announced 2025-04-29 (Caltrans ×2; CDTFA call center
  running on Claude via SymSoft Axyom Assist; State Digital Assistance
  tool piloted at 8 departments; no-cost M365 Copilot pilot).
  https://www.gov.ca.gov/2025/04/29/governor-newsom-deploys-first-in-the-nation-genai-technologies-to-improve-efficiency-in-state-government/
- **CAUTION/refuted-as-popularly-told**: CDTFA's primary sources publish
  NO quantified savings; the circulating "1.5% agent-time / 10,000 more
  calls/year" traces to GovTech reporting of an INITIAL ANALYSIS with
  the calls figure an explicit projection. CA is a strong
  procurement-mechanism story, weak on hard outcomes.

## Utah — enterprise SaaS rollout (Google Gemini), NOT the AI Policy Office

- The Office of AI Policy / Learning Lab (SB 149) is a business-facing
  regulatory sandbox — no employee-adoption numbers; wrong object for
  adoption claims.
- The real story: Gemini for Workspace — pilot of 257 licenses (June
  2024, mandatory responsible-AI training + policy agreement) scaled to
  ~15–16K employees with access by Jan 2025 (~10K active; ~40% adoption
  in months; target 22K). Impact survey (Jul–Aug 2025, 27% response):
  50% of production users self-reported ≥1 hr/week saved; heavy users
  ~3.5 hrs/week. Access/active counts are platform-measured; savings
  self-reported.
  https://workspace.google.com/customers/utah/ ·
  https://innovate-us.org/comms/utahs-journey-implementing-gemini-across-state-government/

## Cross-state synthesis

Four cleanly distinct mechanisms: PA central-platform pilot→expansion ·
NJ training-first in-house build · CA procurement sandbox · Utah
enterprise SaaS with mandatory training gate. All impact numbers
self-reported; all strong numbers are adoption counts. Do not present
any state as having measured productivity outcomes.
